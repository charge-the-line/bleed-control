# Bleed Control — changelog

Bleeding control modeled on the ACS Stop the Bleed® course, in our own words; not affiliated. Plain-language history, newest first. Practice, not certification.

## 0.25.0 — October 11, 2026 — tester release
- Every activity (the lesson, four drills, six stations and nine scenarios) is played to the end with real taps at 390 px before a release.
- CHANGELOG added; the post-pilot backlog recorded in CLAUDE.md.

## 0.24.0 — October 10, 2026
- The scene buttons, the station buttons, a decision card's options and the result row ignore taps for about half a second after they change, so a finger still moving cannot act by accident.
